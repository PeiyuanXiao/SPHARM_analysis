## Supplementary: sensitivity of SP-SPHARM results to the vMF-sKDE bandwidth (h)

The spherical kernel-density bandwidth was fixed at h = 0.35 (concentration kappa = 1/h^2 = 8.16) in the main analysis. To confirm that the SP-SPHARM results do not hinge on this choice, we recomputed the SP-SPHARM power spectra over h in {0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.35, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50} (kappa 4.0-25.0), holding everything else fixed (SVD alignment, 72x36 KDE grid, 64x128 Driscoll-Healy grid, l_max = 20, AC-normalisation). Morphology (M-SPHARM) is independent of h and was reused unchanged. At h = 0.35 the recomputed spectra reproduce the cached production values, so the sweep is anchored to the main pipeline.

**Order selection (truncation at l = 1-6).** 
Across all h, SP-SPHARM power saturates early: cumulative power through l = 6 is 96.71-99.99% (EXP) and the cross-specimen CV first exceeds 100% at degree l = 7-14 (range across h). The l = 1-6 truncation is therefore broadly robust to h.

**Ideal-core discriminability.** 
The pairwise standardised-distance structure is highly stable: each h's distance matrix correlates with the h = 0.35 matrix at Pearson r >= 0.961. Key separations persist at every h (e.g. discoid vs Levallois, biface vs unifacial discoid).

**Experimental cores (PERMANOVA).** 
The scar-pattern PERMANOVA by core type stays significant across h (R2 = 0.259-0.374; p < 0.05 throughout). The pairwise resolution profile is stable: 8 of 10 typology pairs are resolved at h = 0.35.

**Decoupling (Mantel + RV).** 
Morphology and scar patterning remain statistically decoupled at every h: global Mantel r = -0.062--0.008 (EXP) and 0.001-0.017 (SDG), RV = 0.100-0.117 (EXP) and 0.090-0.093 (SDG), see table for significance. The SDG scar~core-type PERMANOVA is significant throughout (R2 = 0.166-0.177).

**Verdict.** h = 0.35 sits in a stable plateau: every headline conclusion (early truncation at l = 1-6, ideal-core discrimination, experimental core-type separation, and morphology-technology decoupling) holds across the full h in [0.20, 0.50] tested.
